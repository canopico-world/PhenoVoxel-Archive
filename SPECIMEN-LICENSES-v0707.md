## Atlantic mantis shrimp — Squilla empusa

DigitalLife Model 105 is an adult-female reconstruction by Johnson Martin with Josh Medina, in collaboration with Dr Kate Feller (Union College) and DigitalLife / UMass Amherst. The author describes a preserved-specimen photogrammetry scan augmented with live-animal photographs and measurements.

- Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/model-105-mantis-shrimp-dc629244fd9a4a2fb2708d0498883500
- Author account: https://johnson-martin.artstation.com/projects/oJWlrB
- License: CC BY-NC 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/
- Derivative: `model-source-squilla-empusa-v0707.glb`. Mesh simplified for interactive viewing; four source parts retained. New vertex pigment and PBR shell finish authored by PhenoVoxel. No source texture images were supplied. Pigment placement is illustrative, not a measured colour record or reconstruction of the source individual's skin. No animation is asserted for this static derivative.
- The derivative retains the attribution and noncommercial restriction. It is not MIT geometry. Exported snapshots retain this asset's license.
- `squilla-editable-v0707.blend` retains the authored vertex pigment and Blender material nodes on the simplified source geometry, under the same CC BY-NC 4.0 restriction. The web GLB uses the portable vertex pigment and PBR finish; Blender-only procedural microrelief is not presented as a web texture map.

## Species boundaries and scientific limits

The separate Japanese mantis-shrimp download represents Oratosquilla oratoria; it has not been substituted for Squilla empusa. Reindeer remains a reference-only record. Sensory/DNA records retain their existing evidence and limits. Photorealism, metric anatomy, colour reflectance, locomotion and physiology have not been independently validated by this release.
