COMPUTATIONAL STUDY 001 · 13 NOVEMBER 2026

How stable is a connectome illustration?

A sensitivity audit of two fly steering pathways, using one openly released male central nervous system.

EXPLORATORY SECONDARY ANALYSIS EXTERNAL REVIEW PENDING

PhenoVoxel research collaboration · Project direction and supervision: PhenoVoxel project supervisor. AI-assisted analysis and internal criticism; no university affiliation is claimed.

THE QUESTION IN EVERYDAY LANGUAGE

Is the highlighted route the whole story?

Imagine a road map with one brightly highlighted journey. It shows a possible route, but it does not tell you where most traffic goes. Our neuron explorer has the same limitation: an attractive chain of connected cells is an example, not a complete explanation of an animal’s movement.

We counted and weighted the eligible routes under a declared set of rules, then changed those rules. The result tests the stability of a graph summary. It does not measure electrical activity or explain what the fly feels.

What the audit found

Across 36 configurations per cell (144 source-by-setting rows), the weighted side preference retained its sign: same-side for both DNa02 cells and opposite-side for both DNg13 cells. The availability-adjusted target-count score also retained its sign. This is robustness within the declared grid, not across every possible connectome analysis.

Magnitude depended strongly on what we counted. For DNg13_L at minimum one synapse, the primary weighted index was +0.969, while the availability-adjusted unique-target index was only +0.072. Here 58 same-side and 66 opposite-side motor somata were reached, despite the strongly unequal weighted scores. Counting each reached cell once gives a different summary from weighting its incoming routes. Neither score is a measured behavioral effect.

The illustrated paths were leading examples, not the whole circuit. At minimum 10, the previously highlighted single paths represented 19.22% (DNa02_R) and 8.78% (DNg13_L) of their eligible routing mass. Their endpoints ranked first, yet accounted for only 27.38% and 15.81% respectively after all eligible routes to each endpoint were included.

Stricter filters discarded much of the included mass. Raising the edge minimum from 1 to 50 retained only 17.48–39.75% in the primary two-edge setting. Removing the largest contributing intermediate preserved the primary side signs. Across the broader deletion sweep, 4 of 96 two-edge cases had no route remaining (DNg13_L at minimum 50, in both endpoint sets and both intermediate restrictions); their laterality is undefined. The other 92 retained the original sign. These are numerical stress tests, not evidence that the biological circuits tolerate cell loss.

What was already known?

Yang and colleagues (2024) used biological experiments and connectomics to distinguish DNa02 and DNg13 contributions to steering. Cheong and colleagues (2026) examined indirect motor connectivity and the same intermediate cell types shown in our earlier explorer. Stürner and colleagues (2025) compared DNa02 partners across sides and connectomes.

Those discoveries belong to the source researchers. Our contribution is a specific, reproducible audit of the MaleCNS illustration across endpoint definitions, edge-count thresholds and intermediate restrictions. A targeted literature search found strong overlap in methods and biology; it does not establish that this parameter sweep is the first of its kind.

Methods you can inspect

We used the MaleCNS v1.0 annotations and complete released connection weights, with source hashes pinned in the plan. Four individual descending cells represent DNa02 and DNg13 on both sides of one specimen. All four retain the release’s “Prelim Roughly traced” status. The source reconstruction is described by Berg and colleagues (2026).

The primary endpoint set contains 135 motor neurons with foreleg annotation and a T1 soma: 68 left and 67 right. The comparator contains all 173 T1 motor somata, including non-foreleg categories. Every motor endpoint lacks a root-side annotation, so cell-body side is the explicitly limited outcome.

For a direct route, the score is the edge count divided by all released outgoing counts from its source. For a two-edge route, multiply those fractions for each edge, then sum eligible routes. Denominators are fixed before filtering; removing weak edges never redistributes their weight onto surviving edges. The score is dimensionless structural routing mass, not a transmission probability, firing rate or force. It differs from published postsynaptic-input-normalized influence measures.

LI = (opposite-side mass − same-side mass) / (opposite-side mass + same-side mass)

Zero total mass gives NA. This sign convention is opposite to an ipsilateral-minus-contralateral index. Unique-target reach is also reported, with adjustment for the unequal number of available endpoints. Direct and two-edge routes remain separate. The full plan specifies exclusions, retained mass, dominant-intermediate removal and example-route checks.

The analysis plan was written after the earlier two example routes had been explored and relevant literature was known, but before the initial sweep’s outcomes were computed. A dated amendment added the literature-motivated threshold 15 after a separate internal calculation had revealed the primary threshold-10 results; that exposure is disclosed in the plan. It is not preregistration. No test animals or held-out biological dataset were collected for this audit.

What this study cannot establish

  • Two homologous cells are not two independent animals. No population 95% confidence interval or significance claim is justified here.
  • Synapse-count thresholds do not test synapse-detection confidence; the source remains at minimum confidence 0.5 throughout.
  • Unsigned connections omit excitation, inhibition, timing, behavioral state, recurrent routes and routes beyond two edges. Excluding other intermediate classes is an analysis choice.
  • Removing one intermediate in the calculation is a sensitivity check, not an experimental neuronal ablation.
  • Incomplete tracing, annotations and score definitions can explain differences. Apparent structural laterality cannot determine steering direction.
  • The previous 17-fly visual-stimulus dataset is a separate experiment. These data do not demonstrate a measured molecule-to-neuron-to-movement causal chain.

Fourteen synthetic checks cover symmetry, missing routes, normalization and other invariants. A separately written internal checker matched eight primary direct/two-edge settings and all 357 required outgoing denominators using the original files. Read the internal review record. AI-assisted critique is not independent scientific peer review. Human specialist review and replication in additional specimens remain necessary.
