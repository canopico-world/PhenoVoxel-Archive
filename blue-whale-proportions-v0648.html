Remaining scientific limits

  • Proportions, fin dimensions and grey-blue colour mapping are authored qualitative choices, not measurements or a specimen fit.
  • Skin mottling remains a locally normalized and recoloured artist atlas. Natural individual variation, underwater scattering, scars, epibionts, wet specular response and deep tissue optics are not reconstructed.
  • Source animation is a finite local fluke/flipper excerpt, not measured full swimming, respiration, feeding, flow, contact force, energetics or physiology.
  • Mouth/baleen, ventral pouch/pleat depth, paired blowhole rims and eye anatomy remain simplified.
  • Final software compatibility views cannot establish working WebGL material/normal-map optics.
  • The caudal/back slope and local rig-bend crease, frontal cross-section, fluke thickness and fin profile remain selected artist geometry rather than fitted living soft tissue.
