Three individual crab corrections

Version 0.6.18 adds three substantially revised records, reaching 257 after verified publication. 43 of the saved drafts remain withheld; 293 records remain to 550. The earlier 254 species objects are unchanged. 144 final actual viewer states were inspected on 18 labelled sheets, with authentic licensed photographs and scoped biological references.

These are partial educational reconstructions, with unmeasured dimensions, cuticle optics and timing. There is no biological certification, independent specialist review, 90% accuracy score or physiological simulation. The next ordered correction is Grapsus grapsus.

Necora puber

A flatter regional shell has five forward side teeth per side, a median frontal incision, paired larger lobes and intervening small denticles. Strong unequal ridged chelae, black cutting edges, red eyes, six pointed supports and two articulated sharp-tipped paddles replace the generic oval and rod limbs.

Attached short dense instanced setae give the brown shell a velvet surface. Chela hair and raised blue/purple ridge cues, darker fingers, regional numerical cuticle pigment and microrelief are retained across actions.

Remaining: Frontal denticle counts vary; the authored positions, widths, shell profile and limb ratios are unmeasured. Pubescence density, ridge granules, mouthparts, ventral plates, gills and pleopods remain simplified. The licensed photo shows an embracing pair; the model is a single supported male morphology study, without mating or moulting.

Eriocheir sinensis

A convex rounded-quadrangular shell has four side teeth with a reduced fourth, four frontal teeth and a V-shaped median notch. Long eight-legged support, narrow pointed final legs, nearly equal hairy palms, white finger tips and a simplified male ventral cover replace the sparse generic draft.

Dense attached short dark claw setae replace scattered hair rods. A smoother dark cuticle with restrained variation, paler limb undersides and distal leg fringes replaces uniform tan pigment.

Remaining: Shell outline, cristae, eye sockets, leg ratios and joint axes remain unmeasured. The hair field is an authored approximation without calibrated density or wetness. Maxillipeds, sternites, gills and pleopods are incomplete. The selected male photograph shows a defensive display; the supported study does not reproduce that display, burrowing or migration.

Hemigrapsus sanguineus

A distinct quadrangular shell has three anterolateral teeth per side, a broad entire front and depressed posterolateral cues. Shorter pointed dactyli, spotted symmetrical palms, an adult-male membranous swelling and higher supported body/claw carriage replace the generic rod-legged body.

Dark subtly varied shell, cream/red-spotted chelae, banded cuticle segments and pale ventral regions replace uniform brown. Numerical microrelief and sparse distal setae are original authored surfaces; no photo pixels are embedded.

Remaining: Exact suborbital stridulatory crest, swelling contour, teeth, joint plates, mouthparts, gills and underside remain incomplete or unmeasured. The selected photo does not independently establish sex; the male swelling has separate morphological support. Band/spot positions, optics and wear remain approximate and stylized at fine scale.
