Palaemon serratus

Original laterally compressed carapace and continuous tapered six-somite abdomen replace the untextured repeated rounded segments. The selected upward-curved rostrum has posterior tooth cues, a distal dorsal gap and bifid-tip cue; second pleural plates overlap the adjacent regions. Two anterior small chelate pairs, with P2 larger than P1, precede three posterior main support pairs. Separate scaphocerites, three antennular flagellar cues, five simple pleopod pairs and a narrow telson with four uropodal lobes are retained.

Original subdued grey-green cuticle, oblique cephalic and vertical abdominal brown bands and limited semitransparency. No source photograph pixels or measured tissue-optics calibration. The continuous cuticle deforms over the existing articulated joints; it does not exchange models between actions.

Remaining: Still partly stylized: transparency, pale overlap regions, fine chromatophore layout, ventral/mouthpart anatomy, fan setae/rays and detailed limb segmentation are incomplete. The selected rostrum and overall proportions are unmeasured. Close diagnostic views deliberately crop some distal antenna/tail portions; neutral views retain the whole organism. No independent taxonomic redetermination or biological validation.

Crangon crangon

Distinct flattened broad cephalothorax with a short unarmed rounded rostrum, continuous tapering abdomen, smooth sixth dorsal somite, stout first-pair subchelae and smaller second-pair chelae. Six posterior main walking limbs support the body. Separate antennal scales, five simple pleopod pairs, a telson and four uropodal lobes replace the same rounded draft used for the prawn.

Original brown/cream fine mottling and darker fan ends replace a flat beige draft. The initial overly pale coarse speckling was reduced. Selected pigmentation is not a calibrated chromatophore state, scan, or photograph texture; fine dorsal segment boundaries and natural optical variation remain weak.

Remaining: Still partly stylized: head cuticle is too smooth in places, mottling and dorsal somite separation remain approximate, and ventral anatomy, fan rays/setae, fine spine positions and actual chromatophore changes are incomplete. Limb segmentation and support order are authored, not species-measured. No swimming trajectory, full escape, feeding or hydrodynamics; no independent biological validation.

Pagurus bernhardus

Original short anterior shield, short ocular stalks, larger right granular chela and smaller left chela replace an undifferentiated draft. Four principal walking limbs are distinct from reduced P4/P5 shell-support cues. A partial soft curled pleon is enclosed by an open helicospiral shelter of undetermined gastropod taxon; that shell is not counted as another species.

Selected cream/orange mottled cuticle with seated granule/spinule cues and greenish eye ends follows the authentic reference qualitatively. The original numeric shell has continuous whorls, a lip, inner wall and subdued worn pigment. No source photo pixels or measured material calibration are used.

Remaining: Still partly stylized: the eye region and mouthparts are simplified, limb segmentation and exact claw allometry are unmeasured, some shell-opening/body relationships remain schematic and shell support/load is not solved. The illustrative shell does not reconstruct the reference individual's identified gastropod species, handedness or dimensions. No independent biological validation or measured gait/physiology.
