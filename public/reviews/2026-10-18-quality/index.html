Evidence and scoring

Ordinal 0–3 by dimension; not an accuracy percentage and not an interval/ratio scale. Do not sum or convert to percent. A score of 2 means partial visible agreement with important limitations; it does not mean 67% accurate. No dimension received the measured-validation level.

  • 0 — Mechanism absent or no credible representation in the assessed scope
  • 1 — Major visible omissions or mismatches require correction
  • 2 — Some visible reference criteria match; significant unmeasured limitations remain
  • 3 — Requires measured multiview/trajectory agreement with stated errors and independent validation; not awarded here

ITIS returned 190 exact strings: 188 valid/accepted and 2 non-accepted entries. 113 contain English vernaculars; 67 current labels match after punctuation/case normalization. Different or missing common names are not automatically errors.

Navigation groups follow sourced taxonomic ranks, with explicit ITIS corrections. Alphabetical siblings are not evolutionary ages or a branching-order claim. Microeukaryotes and legacy Protista/Monera counts are convenience groupings, not modern monophyletic kingdoms.

Changes and material limitations
  • Replaced release-order/Mimosa-first index with domain/kingdom/phylum/class sections and alphabetical lower ranks; full species binomials are primary labels.
  • Added sourced ITIS class/order corrections for turtle, crocodile and both fish; retained original GBIF concepts for comparison.
  • Added a 250-record name/vernacular audit, disclosed ITIS/Kew and spelling/combination conflicts, and withheld three misleading genome-cluster occurrence maps.
  • Corrected tiger walking support/recovery on the retained textured mesh and added a same-mesh wild-colour rabbit coat study.
  • Fixed diagnostic screenshot timing after detecting blank captures; all included sheets must pass a non-uniform-pixel check.
  • The requested 90% biological accuracy has not been established. No specialist credentials, independent review, measured biomechanics, photogrammetry or organism physiology are implied.
  • A single photograph cannot determine all three-dimensional anatomy, population/sex/life-stage variation or a movement cycle. Screenshots and software contact constraints do not constitute biological validation.
  • ITIS is a Smithsonian-partnered resource, not a direct Smithsonian collection determination. Direct institutional name references are individually identified; complete collection/common-name verification remains pending.
  • Generic/common labels vary by source and language. Exact ITIS string matches can be synonyms; absence from a database does not establish invalidity. Species-complex and broad nominal concepts still need specialist resolution.
  • Reference photographs are real, separately credited source images. Authored pigment fields are not photographs. Source model and image licenses/checksums are retained.
  • Previous review links remain available, but are not counted as fresh review in this checkpoint. Source references are traceability links, not a claim that every cited paper was reread in full.
