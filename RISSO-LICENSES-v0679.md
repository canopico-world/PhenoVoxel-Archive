# Risso appearance reference and editable behavior project · 0.6.79

The two views contain different artworks.

Major's **Risso's dolphin (Grampus griseus)** is displayed through its official Sketchfab iframe with creator/platform attribution and a link to the original. Its model ID is `f56e629915c24459897ea8f902bcbcbb`. No Major geometry, material maps or screenshot pixels have been extracted, copied into the editable body or distributed as a downloadable model. The creator retains the original artwork's rights. The embedded viewer remains dependent on Sketchfab and the creator's availability.

The editable behavior surface derives from **ivan.geraldo's Risso dolphin**, https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/e9d97f00de3948218f0388e8a2a117df, under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Its source-body lineage is retained through the archive's credited v0.6.77 and v0.6.78 assets. The original source-file hash remains in the asset record. Blender modifications include a closed remeshed shell, smoothed source rings, integrated eye apertures and closed mouth/blowhole grooves, original pale-head and gray-body skin pigment, flat healed scar artwork, a separate roughness channel, and an editable axial study rig. The coarse earlier normal bake is omitted from the portable skin. The new GLB, Blender project, original rake-mask artwork and renderings of this derivative retain **CC BY 4.0** with source credit and these modification notes.

`risso-editable-v0679.blend` contains the editable surface, packed textures, source notes, four-bone rig and an authored 121-frame study at 24 fps. The static GLB is a lighter neutral derivative with protected eye and mouth samples. Its browser movement is supplied by the archive controller, not by Major's embedded model. Native and browser motion are authored illustrations; their timing is not a measured trajectory. Independent anatomical, biomechanical and visual validation remains pending.

Blender and its bundled rendering tools were used without a paid model or paid modeling service. Original implementation scripts are MIT. To rebuild the native project in a local repository checkout, run `risso-blender-v0679.py`, `risso-polish-v0679.py` and `risso-export-v0679.py` in that order with Blender. The portable GLB's final JPEG compression and vertex welding are recorded in the asset/proof hashes. Blender 5.4 alpha's incomplete bundled glTF exporter is not required by the separate static exporter.

Major's artist model is an appearance reference, not a scientific scan or a validated anatomical specimen. Actual species photographs and biological accounts guide the written record. This release does not establish photorealism, complete feeding physiology, fluid forces, oxygen dynamics or a DNA-to-phenotype relationship. The earlier nine-species skin batch remains under visual review.
