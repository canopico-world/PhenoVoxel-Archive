Five individual coastal crab corrections

Version 0.6.19 reaches 262 records after verified publication. 38 saved drafts remain withheld; 288 remain to 550. Prior 257 species objects are unchanged. 240 actual final model states were inspected on 27 labelled sheets against genuine licensed photographs and scoped biological sources.

Each model remains a partial educational reconstruction, with unmeasured dimensions, cuticle optics and biological timing. No scientific certification, independent specialist review, 90% accuracy or physiological simulation is claimed. Source-access gaps and residual inaccuracies are recorded individually.

Grapsus grapsus

A lower rounded shell, thicker continuous sidewall, widely separated short eye stalks, eight broad flattened articulated walking legs, shorter dark joint caps and paired red chelae with pale distal tips replace the oval and rod-limbed draft.

Selected red marginal, blue central and ochre pigment fields, fine cuticle relief, distal setae and red chelae are original numerical approximations guided by the authentic Galapagos photograph.

Remaining: The regional shell outline, frontal lobes, orbital sockets, flattened limb cross sections and fine mouthparts are incomplete or unmeasured. Front carriage still differs from the selected photo. Pigment placement remains illustrative, with no sex/age determination, measured optics or full natural variation.

Ocypode quadrata

A high quadrangular shell and continuous lateral wall, large dark elongated eye ends on thicker unhorned stalks, asymmetric cream chelae, eight flattened articulated limbs and curved terminal points replace the generic oval and needle appendages.

Sandy yellow cuticle, restrained pale variation, fine granules and leg-edge setae are original numerical surfaces retained through movement.

Remaining: The frontal/orbital region, stridulating ridges, gill chambers, buccal plates and ventral details remain incomplete. Eye and limb proportions, granule density, wetness and sand-dependent coloration are unmeasured. The selected quiet pose does not reproduce the photo's body tilt or its burrow context.

Leptuca pugilator

A distinct broader-than-long shell, narrow long eye stalks, eight banded articulated walking legs, one greatly enlarged flattened male palm and a separate small handling claw replace the symmetric generic body. The major appendage has its own whole-arm articulation.

Muted olive/brown shell variation, darker limb bands, granular major-palm relief and narrow cream fingers replace uniform tan. The selected large-clawed male phenotype does not represent every sex or handedness.

Remaining: Front/orbit contours, exact claw allometry, finger dentition, leg asymmetry, joints and fine feeding setae remain unmeasured or incomplete. The major palm remains simplified. The source-labelled photo was not independently redetermined against sibling Leptuca species. Pattern/optics and waving trajectories are authored, not calibrated.

Pinnotheres pisum

A selected soft adult female study now has a continuously rounded inflated envelope, inconspicuous small eyes, short eight-legged articulation, strongly curved short dactyli, equal small chelae and a broad female ventral cover. Small separate third-maxilliped propodus/dactylus cues replace bead-like mouthparts.

Smooth cream/yellow regional variation and an authored semitransparent material replace the hard-edged oval. The selected photograph's preparation and live optical state are not established.

Remaining: Soft-body deformation, setal filtering structures, gonopores, gills, host fit and complete third-maxilliped anatomy are incomplete. The selected model is unmeasured and still simplified. Translucency is an illustration, without calibrated scattering, visible organs or a physiological claim. The source photo does not independently determine sex or preparation.

Maja brachydactyla

A strongly convex pear-shaped shell with separate median/lateral tapered spines and paired divergent rostral horns, eight long articulated walking limbs, curved terminal toes and longer robust male chelipeds replaces the smooth oval with rods. Male arms now spread laterally instead of pointing together in front.

Brown/red numerical cuticle variation, granular relief and attached short setae on the shell and limbs replace uniform flat pigment. No photographic pixels, encrusting-organism model or measured optical properties are embedded.

Remaining: Detailed orbital/hepatic spine clusters, antennal basal plates, maxillipeds, sternum, gonopods and appendage proportions remain incomplete or unmeasured. The shell and male palms still simplify the photo. Fine hair density, substrate cover and optical response are illustrative. Primary figure pixels could not be obtained in this access; no claim of figure-based geometric measurement is made.
