Three individual clawed-lobster corrections

Version 0.6.20 reaches 265 records after verified publication. 35 saved drafts remain withheld; 285 remain to 550. Prior 262 species objects are unchanged. 165 actual final model states were inspected on 18 labelled sheets against genuine licensed photographs and scoped biological sources.

Each model remains a partial educational reconstruction, with unmeasured dimensions, cuticle optics and biological timing. No scientific certification, independent specialist review, 90% accuracy or physiological simulation is claimed. Source-access gaps and residual inaccuracies are recorded individually.

Homarus gammarus

A continuous robust carapace, unequal naked crusher/cutter palms, shorter crusher fingers, a spiny rostrum without a ventral tooth, eight jointed walking limbs including small P2/P3 chelae, six overlapping abdominal plates and a five-part tail fan replace the oval/rod draft.

Selected dark blue cuticle, restrained cream speckles, reddish palms and antennae, pale seam edges and original curved surfaces remain on the same retained model through all actions.

Remaining: Exact body ratios, rostral teeth, orbital/buccal plates, cutting dentition, joint axes, telson/uropod struts and setal fields remain unmeasured or incomplete. Palms, dorsal grooves and pleurae are still simplified. First sex-modified pleopods are not reconstructed. The source photo does not establish sex, optics or the entire species' variation.

Homarus americanus

A separately proportioned robust carapace, a ventral rostral tooth, one broader crusher and a longer-fingered cutter, eight supported articulated walking limbs with small P2/P3 claws, six serial abdominal plates and a central telson with four uropod lobes replace the generic draft.

A warm red/olive selected phenotype follows the authentic lit NOAA/NURP source image. Smooth naked palms, fine varied cuticle and pale seams replace the undifferentiated tan surface; no generated photograph or measured material map is used.

Remaining: Rostral tooth variability, fine cuticular detail, setal structures, mouthparts, joint axes, claw dentition and internal physiology remain incomplete/unmeasured. The red/olive selection does not represent all natural coloration, and the photograph cannot calibrate illumination or reflectance. Sex/age are not independently redetermined; modified first pleopods are omitted.

Nephrops norvegicus

A slender ridged carapace, authored kidney-shaped eye ends, three lateral rostral spine pairs with a ventral tooth cue, unequal elongated ridged palms, four pairs of jointed walking limbs with small P2/P3 chelae, six sculptured abdominal plates and a separate five-part fan replace the generic lobster.

Dorsal orange and a lighter underside, longitudinal palm/carapace ridge cues, small spine rows and pale serial seam margins are original numerical cuticle surfaces. The selected captive photograph guides the orange/cream appearance without calibrating material properties.

Remaining: Fine eye contour, rostral variation, spine/denticle positions, setal density, scaphocerite shape, detailed grooves and joints remain approximate. The mild selected abdominal arch does not reproduce every captive/wild posture. First sex-modified pleopods and internal organs are incomplete; the source photo does not independently establish sex or stage.
