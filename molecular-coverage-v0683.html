What you can study

Rotate the local DNA schematic, select A/C/G/T or ambiguous bases, search both strands, inspect source gene annotations and preview clearly labelled hypothetical substitutions. Translation follows each CDS’s deposited genetic code. Uncertain or context-dependent translations are withheld. Download reference FASTA and source annotation evidence.

Match the NCBI species name or documented synonym; source taxon must be that species or a verified descendant. Prefer complete mitochondrial RefSeq records for animals, plastid markers for plants, ribosomal DNA for fungi and microbes. Only deposited DNA intervals of 200–35,000 bp are bundled. This is not a claim to supply complete nuclear genomes or all available sequences.

The sequence is not the genotype of the photographed or rendered individual. The helix is a diagram, not molecular dynamics. This release does not perform sequencing, read alignment, variant calling, or validated prediction of traits or disease.
