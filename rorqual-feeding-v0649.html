The previously sealed mouths now open through cranial elevation and mandibular depression, with the retained outer pouch expanding, closing and contracting more slowly. This is a qualitative external movement study. It does not establish scientifically validated biomechanics, complete feeding physiology or photorealism.

How to inspect

Choose Lunge feeding study in either whale, then use the six stage buttons. Pause at opening, maximum gape, closed but distended pouch, draining and recovered hold. Side, front, top and mouth/pouch anatomy views show the same skin, atlas and added oral surfaces throughout. The finite studies stop; they do not loop rapidly through feeding.

Authentic whole references

These photographs are separate from the artist models. They are not generated, cropped into a texture atlas or treated as complete motion records. Splash, water, perspective and occlusion limit interpretation. The two humpback frames mainly show exterior lips/pleats near the surface, not the whole submerged pouch or a complete gape cycle.
