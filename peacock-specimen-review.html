Peacock mantis shrimp

Odontodactylus scyllarus

The paired folded clubs, stalked eyes and segmented abdomen are a static reconstruction guided by whole-animal CT views and a species photograph. Surface colours are authored. Dimensions, pigment reflectance and joint motion have yet to be calibrated against an individual.

What each source documents

  • DigiMorph · whole animal CT views
    Gross external anatomy and burrowing ecology. CT voxels were not incorporated into the model.
  • GBIF accepted species
    Species identity and classification.
  • Cédric Péneau · Réunion specimen
    External colour and markings in visible light.
  • Chiou et al. · circular polarization discrimination
    Feeding-tube choice under matched opposite circular polarization.
  • Kleinlogel & Marshall · ultraviolet-sensitive cells
    Species-specific R8 photoreceptor recordings.
  • GenBank · PP761321.1
    Partial mitochondrial genome from voucher IU-2013-236. Gene coordinates and translations follow the deposited annotation. The retinal opsin transcripts linked in Evidence come from separate records.
  • GenBank · Os1 partial opsin mRNA
    A separate transcript accession. A deposited transcript alone does not establish a behavioural colour channel.
