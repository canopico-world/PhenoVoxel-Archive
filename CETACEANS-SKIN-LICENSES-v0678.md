# Cetacean skin and facial corrections — v0.6.78

Original source-body grants are preserved. No non-downloadable Sketchfab geometry or screenshot texture was extracted. All ten GLBs contain replacement albedo, normal and roughness maps, corrected eyes/lids, skin-covered native jaws (authored lips on Risso), and closed blowhole surfaces. Body UV charts were repacked to remove overlaps. Risso's muzzle was rounded; pilot pectorals were extended. Original artist rig clips remain unused. Movement is authored on the baked exterior. Material maps are reconstructions and not photographic scans.

The four exterior sheets and close-up/movement sheets use archive-rendered images of these licensed derivatives. Sheets containing Caperea or Omura include CC BY-NC 4.0 elements. Reference photos remain at their original linked hosts with their original credits and licenses.
