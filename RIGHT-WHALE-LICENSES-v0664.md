# Northern right-whale derivatives · 0.6.64

The North Atlantic (*Eubalaena glacialis*) and North Pacific (*Eubalaena japonica*) models derive from **Model 55A - Southern Right Whale**, by the **Digital Life project, University of Massachusetts Amherst**, with CG artist **Robert Gutierrez**. The Southern source is a reconstruction of *Eubalaena australis*, rather than a northern specimen.

- Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/model-55a-southern-right-whale-3bddfdfafc6c43758df9b94bc9e0fe9e
- Source grant retained for these derivatives: **CC BY-NC 4.0**, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/
- Research/artist lineage: https://www.whoi.edu/press-room/news-release/researchers-use-drones-to-weigh-whales/
- Digital Life access policy: https://digitallife3d.org/sample-models

Northern interpretation and changes by **PhenoVoxel Archive**: baked source pose, modest selected adult body/head shape changes, new non-overlapping UV charts, new numeric albedo/normal/roughness maps, irregular head-local callosity relief, and finite local movement studies. The source skeleton and creator animation are omitted. Dark eye geometry and hidden closed-mouth baleen remain. The original Southern atlas pixels are omitted from the northern textures. Source and delivered checksums are recorded in `right-whale-source-v0664.json`.

These derivative models retain the source noncommercial restriction. Original repository code remains under its existing MIT grant. The repository's code/data grants do not override the source model or photograph grants. The original Southern model file and record are unchanged.

## Authentic reference photographs

The two website photograph files are preserved whole, copied without pixel edits to names used by the review page, and credited independently of the new 3D skin maps. No photograph pixels are baked into the models.

- **North Atlantic**: NOAA Photo Library, “A right whale cow and calf.” Public domain, US Government NOAA work. The archive's delivered image was already a cropped source frame; this release preserves that entire delivered frame. Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Anim1750_-_Flickr_-_NOAA_Photo_Library_(cropped).jpg · Grant: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Template:PD-USGov-NOAA
- **North Pacific**: Mark Hoffman and Bruce Long, “20170505 NPRW - Kelping 13.” **CC BY-SA 4.0**. The full delivered head photograph is preserved without cropping or generated changes. Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:20170505_NPRW_-_Kelping_13.jpg · Grant: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

The reference-derived patterns are selected illustrations. This work does not identify either photographed individual in a scientific catalogue, establish calibrated specimen dimensions, or create a universal species-specific callosity pattern.

## Local free tooling

The new atlases were made locally with watlas 1.0.1 / xatlas (https://github.com/toji/watlas), using the already installed task-local package. Skin fields and surface changes are numerical authoring work. No paid-generation service, credits or private uploaded photographs were used.
