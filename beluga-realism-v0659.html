Beluga whale: a less simplified exterior

The previous version was rejected by the user as cartoon-like. This follow-up changes the head, muzzle, eyes, lips, flipper sections, skin and lighting. All 337 species remain in the archive.

This is an authored reconstruction. It is not a living scan, a measured individual or a validated physiological model. The available local browser uses software compatibility lighting; WebGL optical appearance and photorealism have not been established.

What changed

  • Retained the untouched CC0 starting file. Added denser anterior control stations to the resculpted surface, a rounded cap and a fuller, distinct melon/short rostrum/neck profile. Removed the former zero-at-pole axial bump that folded nose rings back through one another.
  • Rebuilt pectorals as swept solid cambered foils with full roots and thinner trailing edges. Revised the fluke leading/trailing contours. New layered small eyes and tapered skin eyelids replace dot-like eyes and tube lids; lip overlays removed in favour of a continuous shallow skin groove.
  • New original cream-grey skin, fine normal and variable roughness fields with sparse authored pale marks. Appendages use body-position UV coordinates so facial tones do not appear on flippers. Reduced beluga fill/rim lighting leaves the surface volume visible.
  • Existing bounded posterior stroke, glide, neck, turning, melon and separate feeding/oral actions retained and checked against the revised geometry. Selected36peg teeth remain an authored anatomy display. No measured individual, living scan, tissue mechanics, pressure/prey/water solver or scientifically validated motion.
  • Recessed and curved the oral floor, palate and tongue, adding restrained original vertex colour variation and posterior depth tones. The internal display remains simplified and unvalidated.

Whole references and evidence
