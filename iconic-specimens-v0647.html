Blue whale is the first reviewed addition in this release;126 nominees remain.

Siberian/Bengal tiger and grizzly/Kodiak bear labels do not become duplicate species. Eastern/western lowland gorilla labels are recorded under their species, with subspecies phenotype scope. London plane is a hybrid, excluded from the 550 species target. Broad common names have explicit provisional representatives; source conflicts remain visible.

These rows record name/concept triage, not completed realistic models. GBIF matches do not determine photographs or replace specialist taxonomy. MDD common names apply to its mammal concepts; plants still need source-by-source specialist verification. The blue whale has a Smithsonian educational-name check; no all-list Smithsonian collection audit is claimed. Accepted names, image identity, morphology and mechanism review remain separate gates.

Future plant development requires distinct source-supported seed, juvenile leaf and reproductive stages. Conifers produce cones; they must not acquire flowers. A mature flowering tree will not simply be scaled up as germination.
