What this pass changed

  • Preserved all 250 v0.6.12 record objects and published quality code during integration.
  • Withheld unsupported mature-form growth for all fifteen new gymnosperm drafts, including direct model/API calls; no seed-to-tree animation was substituted.
  • Corrected meaningful motion sampling: excluded near-zero antenna endpoints, added maximum local sweeps and top-view chela/diatom comparisons.
  • Flagged the gazami crab soft-404 source response and linked separate official supplementary morphology.

These changes are preserved in the unpublished integration branch. They do not turn the rejected drafts into public catalogue records. The earlier sunflower/Arabidopsis and abalone corrections remain live.

Review method and limits

Actual retained-model WebGL2 captures: 150 neutral side/front/top views, 40 local-action side endpoints, 13 top chela endpoints and 4 diatom sliding views. All fifty referenced images and all displayed comparison/motion panels were visually screened. Cached institutional/specialist excerpts and selected live source pages were read; this is not a complete primary-literature or independent taxonomic/biomechanics validation. Maximal local action times are authored display seconds, not biological rates. Qualitative rubric records concrete defects rather than a fabricated percentage.

  • All fifty draft additions fail the current realism release gate; they are not public catalogue records.
  • The existing 250 public educational reconstructions are not certified for quantitative research; their earlier residual issues remain.
  • Protista and Monera are legacy reporting categories; modern taxonomic affiliations and Bacteria/Archaea remain separate.
  • Source-labelled images, names and common names are not independent specimen identifications.
  • No physiology, measured rates, calibrated material optics, expert panel or 90 percent accuracy established.

Initial antenna samples near zero deflection were replaced with meaningful endpoints. Top chela views were added because side views hide the opening plane. Two truncated diatom image exports were replaced and decoded before review. Discarded samples do not inflate the final 207-frame count. This is one initial screen of fifty drafts, not a complete re-review or correction of every published organism.
