VERSION 0.6.28 · 25 OCTOBER 2026

Three large-animal studies

New initial body, texture and swimming comparisons for the killer whale, whale shark and white shark. 3 of the requested 50 new large animals are in this release. The remaining 47 nominations require individual research and model work.

305 total species records, 39 actual final rendered states and six credited comparison sheets. No 90% accuracy, photoreal completion, independent specialist validation or physiological simulation is claimed.

Corrections made during review

The older shark material extension initially hid their pigment. Supported PBR derivatives restore it without replacing source anatomy or UVs. Authored orca and whale-shark posterior movement uses different planes; white shark retains the creator’s Swimming clip. Aquatic clearance, neutral-normal restoration and detail-camera framing were corrected. Repeated near-identical cycle samples were replaced with distinct phases.

Development and movement across the archive

The initial 302 records still contain 153 unavailable mechanisms. These three new records add three explicitly unavailable feeding mechanisms: 156 unavailable behavior records total. They have not been renamed complete. The full inventory also identifies partial studies and legacy records without an explicit rendering assessment.
