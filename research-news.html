THE RESEARCH PROCESS
  1. 01
    Ask a precise question

    Make the claim small enough to challenge.

  2. 02
    Trace the evidence

    Keep the organism, specimen and source identifiable.

  3. 03
    Try to break the result

    Compare alternatives and report where they fail.

  4. 04
    Publish what can be checked

    Share methods, results, code and remaining uncertainty.

A computational result becomes useful when someone else can inspect and challenge it.

HOW TO READ THESE NOTES

The status matters as much as the headline.

A software test, an anatomical reconstruction and a behavioral experiment answer different questions. We keep their claims separate.

COMPUTATIONAL STUDY

A result from data

A defined analysis of an identified dataset. Look for comparisons, sensitivity checks, limitations and reproducible outputs.

QUESTION / PROTOCOL

A plan to investigate

A question or proposed method. It is not evidence that the proposed result is true.

SOFTWARE UPDATE

A change you can use

A new feature or correction. Passing software tests does not establish biological or physiological validity.

REVIEW & CORRECTIONS

An open record

We identify internal AI-assisted checks separately from external review, and retain dated corrections when conclusions change.

PEOPLE, TOOLS & RESPONSIBILITY

PhenoVoxel research collaboration

Human-supervised, AI-assisted.

Project direction and supervision: PhenoVoxel project supervisor. AI tools assist with literature searches, analysis, implementation and internal criticism. Each study identifies its actual methods and source authors; this collaboration claims no university affiliation or independent peer-review status.

Supervision does not imply review or approval of an unfinished study.
