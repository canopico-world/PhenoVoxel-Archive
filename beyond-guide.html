Beyond Experience

Open Beyond QR on the screen you’re using. Scan its code with your phone’s Camera app and tap the link. Leave the archive open on both screens.

Show my pairing QR code

Drag a model, photo or six-dot handle to a screen edge. The copy enters from the opposite edge of the other screen. On a touchscreen, hold a text section for two seconds to lift it.

For camera calibration, open Beyond QR on the receiving phone and choose Change screen position. Photograph the four markers on the first screen, or choose Left or Right.

If a connection drops, scan again. Try the same Wi-Fi on both devices if pairing stalls. Use End pairing when you finish.

The public archive uses PeerJS to connect the browsers. The QR invitation expires after 30 minutes of inactivity. Keep it private. The calibration photo stays on the phone.
