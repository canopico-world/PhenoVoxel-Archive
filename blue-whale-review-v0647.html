The whole NOAA eastern-Pacific adult frame supports anterior shape and mottling comparison. The separate NOAA head frame shows paired blowholes and the raised splashguard. Neither is a complete fluking sequence. The grey-blue appearance varies with individual, water, illumination and camera.

Evidence and remaining work

  • Body, rostrum, flipper and fluke proportions are selected, not fitted to an identified individual or subspecies.
  • Source pigment is an artist atlas with disclosed smoothing/colour modulation; natural variation, wet optics, scars and epibionts remain incomplete.
  • Ventral pleats, paired blowhole rims, eyelids, selected eye proportions and internal mouth/baleen anatomy need further geometric reconstruction.
  • The finite creator-derived excerpt is not a complete swimming/feeding/breathing or biomechanical controller; no measured amplitude, period, force or physiological validation.
  • Final software compatibility images cannot validate WebGL normal maps, wet specular response or refraction.
