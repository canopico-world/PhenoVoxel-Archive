Whales and dolphins: 29 species, 30 exterior studies

New original educational reconstructions, not certified anatomical scans or photorealistic specimens. Each has its own selected body controls and a licensed reference photograph. Finite local fluke/posture studies preserve the same geometry, UVs and pigment. Full swimming trajectories, feeding, flow, forces, acoustics and physiology remain unfinished.

All three right-whale species, both minke whales, both pilot whales, pygmy and dwarf sperm whales, narwhal, beluga, Risso’s and Amazon river dolphins are included. Additional dolphins are common, spinner, striped and Irrawaddy; deeper-diving studies include Cuvier’s, northern bottlenose and Blainville’s beaked whales. The blue-whale and humpback feeding studies from v0.6.50 are preserved.

Names, specimens and counts

Following the SMM April 2026 list, coastal Eden and offshore Bryde are selectable studies within one broad Balaenoptera edeni accession. MDD recognizes a separate B. brydei; WoRMS records that name as taxon inquirendum. Neither the authored proportions nor the two source-associated photos independently resolve narrow species identity. The common-dolphin accession likewise follows SMM’s broad concept; its Californian frame may represent a long-beaked form. Family alternatives do not create extra species.

There are 337 release records: 158 Animalia, 87 Plantae, 46 Fungi, 30 legacy Protista and 16 legacy Monera (13 Bacteria, 3 Archaea). That is 29 additions to the previous 308, not 30 new species. Publication and exact public verification are recorded separately. The scheduled continuation remains deleted; this work is manual.

What was compared

Actual whole licensed photographic frames were inspected, with source captions/community identifications kept distinct from independent redetermination. Some rare-species pictures show only a back, fin or head. Expert external-anatomy accounts and actual selected FAO 1993 plates supply separate comparative context; historical taxonomy and conservation estimates are not imported as current facts. The complete Omura specialist image panel was viewed privately after publisher-image access failed. Private paper/guide figures are not redistributed.

Four acquired reusable artist/museum payloads were screened and rejected as ready realistic living bodies: a stylized sperm-whale school asset, hand-painted bowhead, rare two-tusk narwhal and a gray-whale clay sculpture/stand. Fin-whale stranding/dissection and skull leads were unsuitable. No exact-species scan or source artist mesh was adopted. These new numerical skin fields contain no photographic pixels; natural irregularities, tissues and wet optical response remain simplified.

Movement evidence and limits

Gough et al. 2021 used tags and drone morphometry on six rorqual species. Actual selected methods and result text were read: routine versus pre-lunge tailbeats were distinguished; mouth-open/filtration tailbeats were excluded. Those data are not these body trajectories or a general feeding controller. The lone tagged sei whale is not a universal model. Martín López et al. 2015 supplies abstract-level tag evidence for gait switching in three beaked-whale species; new full figures/video were not viewed. The 2022 sperm-whale study provides selected stroke/glide context, not fitted local excursions. Ayancik, Fish and Moored 2020 compares five fluke shapes in an idealized virtual-body/potential-flow setting; its prescribed controls are not universal dolphin playback rates. Only beluga among the present new taxa is in that five-species shape set; other uses are explicitly related-taxon context.

Displayed cycles last twelve authored seconds, ease from and back to neutral, and stop. The four stroke-to-held-tail studies and two local head turns illustrate limited articulation. Root position stays fixed. No measured forward speed, swimming path, thrust, buoyancy, oxygen budget, prey capture or pressure/flow solution is claimed.

Screenshot review

282 actual final compiled states currently saved across 30 model-only sheets. All saved final states have been individually inspected. Final rendering is software compatibility, with simplified lighting; no final WebGL optical review is claimed. Side/front/top views, selected action phases, same-surface reset and head-region views expose remaining defects. A camera/pose is not a biological measurement.

Original dirty-source files, the earlier 308 species objects and their retained artist/photo payloads are preserved. Occurrence snapshots for these new accessions were not verified; the first fin-whale requests failed TLS access and the subsequent batch uses unavailable null counts with maps withheld. No missing count is represented as zero.

Software verification history

The first full local run passed 346 of 352 checks. It found an unavailable-action timing format, the detailed-surface integration marker, historical cohort counts and the expanded current action inventory. Those were repaired with the original historical hashes retained, and an independent exact digest now protects all 308 prior records. The subsequent full local run passed 353 of 353 checks using one test worker and a bounded heap; the published workflow retains its normal test configuration. Earlier focused geometry runs exposed nonperiodic UV-seam relief on sperm and right whales; canonical duplicate seam positions repaired the closed skin. Initial syntax and draft shape/surface defects were corrected before these accepted images. These are software checks, not biological validation. The first owner-browser submission was rejected as too large before any commit; the same 282 PNGs are packaged in two smaller archives.
