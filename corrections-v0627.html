Two new ascomycete studies

Version 0.6.27 adds Xylaria polymorpha and Xylaria hypoxylon after individual primary-reference and actual screenshot review. Verified publication brings the archive from 300 to 302 records; 248 additional species remain to reach 550.

Twenty-six actual final states on four credited comparison sheets. Selected mature external bodies and explicitly prepared sections remain partly stylized and unmeasured. These are initial author reviews, not completed photorealism, independent biological validation, measured spore discharge or a life-cycle simulation. Earlier 300 species objects are preserved exactly.

Xylaria polymorpha

Selected thick, rounded and irregular clavate stromata are distinct from slender X. hypoxylon. The first draft had pointed caps and conspicuous horizontal corrugations that disagreed with the photographed cortex. Rounded dome transitions and reduced radial oscillation corrected those defects. A prepared section is visibly open toward the default camera.

Dark numerical grain and small ostiolar papilla cues give a rougher cortex. The primary section photographs show immersed perithecia rather than protruding black eggs: the first 3D lump cues were replaced by flat section-wall cues with pale lumina, and their clearance was corrected after crescent-shaped occlusion was seen.

Remaining: The clubs and contextual wood remain too regular and partly stylized. Fine cortex cracking, perithecial placement, basal mycelium, scattering and moisture response are incomplete. Section walls are flat ring cues, not reconstructed flask-shaped cavities, asci or actual tissue thickness. Their placement and count are authored. Close anatomy views show partial regions and clip some whole-body extent; the front view occludes other stromata. No developmental, discharge, airflow or physiological mechanism is computed.

Xylaria hypoxylon

A narrow, long-stiped mature study is distinct from broad X. polymorpha. The initial pair of symmetrical long pointed forks resembled regular antlers; one fork was removed, remaining fertile branches were shortened, thickened and made unequally nodulose, and abrupt shaft scaling was removed. A prepared section is explicitly separate from a white conidial appearance.

Dark furrowed cortex and fine selected fertile papilla cues distinguish the mature exterior from pale cut tissue. The section-wall correction removes protruding black lump cues and later crescent occlusion. The complete authentic plate retains its different prepared and intact panels and original scale bars.

Remaining: Branch junctions intersect rather than reproduce a measured continuous tissue union; the remaining fork is still regular. Basal hairs, exact nodules, conidiophores, perithecial walls and ascus microanatomy are incomplete. The prepared white core and ring walls are diagrammatic and unmeasured. Material response, hair density, natural stages, spore dimensions and flow are not calibrated. Close foci are partial anatomy views, and the edge-on front view occludes structures. This selected mature study is not a complete species-complex identification or life-cycle simulation.
