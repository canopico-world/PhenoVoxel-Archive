Original downloaded archive and source files are preserved locally.
Changes: removed the unrelated12-triangle Cube and source camera/light nodes; reduced source2,345,365 animal triangles to1,038,399 with locked primitive borders; preserved six source photographic maps, resized web copies to max3072 pixels/JPEG quality74; retained original material colours, metallicity and roughness. Gzip transport is split into two files; each part and the decompressed GLB have SHA256 verification. No vertex pigment or artificial rainbow palette is added. Blender is used for source inspection, exported-model review and the editable working file.
The first aggressive simplification caused texture artifacts and was rejected; it is not the integrated asset.
The source is static. No movement, physiology, measured scale, calibrated reflectance or subjective visual experience is validated.
DigitalLife Model105 remains Squilla empusa under its existing CC BY-NC4.0 license. Its new50,021,560-byte GLB also contains no image textures or vertex-colour attributes. No species-specific sensory parameters or DNA were copied between taxa.
Japanese species DNA: NC_014342.1,15783bp, NCBI taxon337810. The source XML, sequence hash and deposited annotation restrictions are preserved. This is a separate deposited mitochondrial genome, not the photographed individual's genotype or its full nuclear genome.
