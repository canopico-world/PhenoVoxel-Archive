DigitalLife identifies the reconstructed animal as subadult male Mosi (TZ209), filmed near Mafia Island, Tanzania, November2022. The Marine Megafauna Foundation coordinated and supported the work. Chris Rohner and Stephanie Venables provided footage and support. Jon Bayuk supported model construction and other costs.

The official free4k GLB was downloaded through the signed-in Sketchfab download UI. Source geometry, normals, tangents, UVs, weights, joints, inverse bind matrices and the complete Swim Cycle remain unchanged. This is an artist reconstruction from footage, not independent certification of all biological dimensions or physiology.

Changes by PhenoVoxel Archive: source albedo color response and fine detail revised while preserving source spot layout and countershading; source normal detail combined with restrained continuous surface relief; nonmetallic roughness rebuilt; separate eye response, local display normalization, studio lighting, viewer integration, reviewed renders and documentation. Source animation is retained at its native display rate; no live-animal kinematic fit is claimed. Revised model and derived maps retain CC BY-NC4.0 terms.

The previously registered CanYuTsai source and its payload remain unchanged for historical provenance; they are superseded in the active whale-shark viewer. The existing reference photograph by Daniel Benák retains its separate CC BY4.0 grant and credit. No account information, private photos or credentials are bundled.
