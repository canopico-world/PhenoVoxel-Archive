# Sculpted cetacean surfaces, v0.6.52

Five retained species receive distinct licensed artist surfaces. The other new cetaceans still require deeper reconstruction. The blue whale and humpback surfaces and feeding studies are preserved. This is a partial quality release, not completion of the human request or independent biological validation.
