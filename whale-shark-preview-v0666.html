A fish built for filtering

A broad, flattened head carries a short transverse mouth. Five gill openings lie on each side, and longitudinal ridges run along the body. Whale sharks filter food from water; this viewer preserves the source exterior and shows swimming, while its filtering mechanism remains outside the simulation.

Pattern and skin

The source spots, pale bars and countershading are retained. Revised color response, fine normal detail and variable roughness give the skin a more natural surface under studio light. The detail suggests skin texture rather than a measured microscopic denticle surface.
