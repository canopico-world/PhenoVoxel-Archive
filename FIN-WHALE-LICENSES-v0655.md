The actual user-supplied ZIP is delivered unchanged as fin-whale-source-v0655.zip. It contains source/whale.obj and six original2048×2048PNG maps. Original camera photographs or a source rig were not supplied. The supplied archive corresponds to the creator metadata/control counts, naming and rendered exterior; it is not a verified living-body scan.

Derived model-fin-whale-woelper.glb: two Catmull–Clark subdivision levels, one selected uniform scale/rigid orientation, image-origin UV conversion, and one disclosed albedo derivative. Three active original PNG payloads (normal/roughness/AO) remain byte-exact; the original albedo, metalness and curvature remain in the source archive. Biological skin is rendered nonmetallic. Brightness/saturation adjustment and a selected dark anatomical-left lower-jaw field correct the earlier near-black display and symmetrical source lower-jaw colour; this is not reflectance calibration, measured pigment or a fitted individual. The original artist map is not claimed to be an organism photograph.

The same runtime body and its UV maps perform authored finite posterior bending, glide, orientation and selected jaw/pouch studies. The oral split/lining, selected300plates per rack, deformation masks and timing are archive additions. NOAA describes260–480plates per side and50–100throat pleats; selected display density is not a measured specimen. Complete flow, forces, filtration, prey transport and swallowing are not implemented.

The nine attached user photographs are private comparison material; at least one carries Michael Valos/Blue Planet Archive copyright and the others have no reusable grant here. They are not redistributed or projected onto the body. Whole openly reusable profile/NOAA references retain their existing exact creators/licenses and separate actual download/delivery hashes in CETACEAN-NEXT-FIVE-LICENSES-v0653.md. No new original-resolution photograph retrieval is claimed.
