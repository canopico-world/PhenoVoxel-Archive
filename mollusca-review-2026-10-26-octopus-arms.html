Models are authored studies. Real photographs depict different individuals/poses; comparisons are qualitative and not fitted anatomical or optical measurements.

  • Selected asymmetric longer arm paths with closed rounded tip caps and seam-normal continuity replace the straighter short spread.
  • A closed connected mantle-wall/head/crown field joins eye bulges and basal tissue. Selected local membrane/arm transitions remain approximate.
  • The external funnel has a cleared route into an illustrative mantle-wall cavity. A rejected draft hid the funnel behind basal arms; placement and clearances were revised.
  • Original 512² cellular pigment/height fields and bind-space three-planar mapping add finer surface detail that stays attached during deformation. No source photographs or paper figures became texture pixels.

This remains an unmeasured, partly stylized external adult study. The asymmetric spread is one authored inspection posture, not a fitted resting individual. Selected arm lengths, distal curling/taper, web thickness and unions, head/eye position, iris/cornea, mantle wall/cavity, papilla distribution and pigment/optical diversity are approximate. The retained 38 sucker pairs per arm, radial grooves and short orifices do not reconstruct complete acetabula or fine circumferential/microscopic tissue. A cleared illustrative funnel route does not establish internal anatomical fidelity, valves, respiratory organs, pressure or water flow. Proximal extension/radius adjustment and a travelling bend are authored mechanisms, not measured strains or a muscular-hydrostat solver. Selected distal support regions compensate for body translation; suction, mucus/traction, force equilibrium, whole-body and between-sample contact/collision freedom, complete crawling, prey handling and jet escape remain unavailable. Coded finite sequences are not an inferred motor-controller or measured cadence. Skin fields are original numerical artwork, not photograph/SEM pixels, camouflage physiology or calibrated natural optics. Compatibility/export uses restrained coarse numerical grain instead of the main cellular shader detail, with reduced fidelity. No 90-percent score, independent specialist review or biological validation is claimed.

Evidence and scope

  • Hanassy et al.2015 · Stereotypical reaching movements involve bend propagation and elongation

    The actual PubMed abstract describes 3D reaching reconstructions and variable proximal elongation in nominal Octopus vulgaris. Only selected conceptual mechanisms are illustrated; no trajectories, strain values, fitted forces, full paper figures or video were obtained.

  • Levy and Hochner 2017 · Embodied organization of morphology, vision and locomotion

    Actual publisher PDF printedpages 3–4 Figure 1 andFigure 2 were rendered/inspected, with selected text. They show irregular recruitment/contact records and aquarium frames/eye roll in nominal Octopus vulgaris; Figure 1 also includes insect comparisons. This is a perspective, not a newly retrieved primary 2015 full dataset or fitted control model. No source figures are redistributed.

  • Allen et al.2014 · Comparative morphology of changeable skin papillae

    Selected author-hosted PDF text and indexed abstract were read. A new figure download/render did not succeed. The study distinguishes several cephalopod species and prepared tissue, including nominal O.vulgaris small dorsal/ventral eye papillae from Florida; modern common-octopus-complex identity is not redetermined. No other species compound papillae, measured microanatomy or internal muscles are copied into this surface. Cellular pigment/relief is original numerical artwork guided qualitatively by authentic photographs.

Actual 2017 publisher PDF printedpages 3–4 and Figures 1–2 were inspected. Its aquarium motion frames are distinct from a newly obtained primary 2015 dataset. Selected Hanassy 2015 abstract supports the separate reach concepts. The Allen comparative paper has several species and prepared tissues; no new figure inspection succeeded. The nominal Florida O.vulgaris material has a modern species-complex limitation.

Initial preview exposed overly uniform curling, coarse/faceted core relief and a funnel hidden by basal tissues. Closed-core checks found 60 open boundary edges at a clipped construction bound; the domain was enlarged, retaining the closed/connected gate. An authored recovery path produced a sampled distal arm 6/7 overlap; selected curvature was changed and the complete sampled finite interval rechecked. A new cap test initially used an incorrect absolute-height assumption for proximal rings; it was corrected to test membership of the actual proximal boundary while retaining exactly 32 boundary edges. A metadata check had a case-sensitive wording mismatch, corrected without changing biological status. A 33 frame compiled candidate was withheld after its inspected underside detail exposed proximal sucker faces hidden by core stubs; flattened basal tissue and a new selected lip-exposure gate corrected that regression before full recapture. Six software-fallback drafts exposed UV-stretched cellular dots and were rejected; UV grain replaced those fields in the standard material while the custom main shader stayed unchanged. All 33 ordered main recaptures were byte-identical to the inspected final PNGs, and six new fallback Observe axis/detail captures were inspected. The corrected geometry passed 256 full local checks before this final material-only change;16 affected checks passed after it. Full CI on the published source is checked separately during release verification. All earlier draft captures are private and excluded from final counts.

Mesh topology, open route, retained geometry/reset, selected support, sampled distal envelopes and numerical-source reproducibility are software consistency checks. They do not validate living anatomy, physiology or full contact mechanics.
