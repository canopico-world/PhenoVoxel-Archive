Anthozoa and box jellyfish: individual exterior corrections

Version 0.6.44 · October 27, 2026 · all 16 existing Anthozoa: 11 anemone profiles and 5 corals/sea fans, followed by Chironex fleckeri. 307 species remain public; this quality release adds zero.

These are source-supported authored exterior studies, with smoother variable tentacles, more distinct bodies and original surface pigment. They remain partly stylized and unmeasured. No photographed skin was invented or projected onto the model. Tissue optics, natural variation, full feeding, propulsion and physiology remain incomplete. Software compatibility screenshots do not establish final WebGL optical fidelity or biological validation.

102 actual previous public views were individually compared with whole credited photographs. 153 final compiled views were individually inspected. No accuracy percentage or independent specialist credentials are asserted.

How the comparisons were made

Each record was examined separately for gross shape, surface pattern/relief, tentacle placement and support during the implemented local action. The same surfaces and attached colour field remain through the eight-second study and recover to neutral. Counts and timing are authored selections. Each source below identifies what was actually read or seen; a downloaded page or paper is not a claim to have inspected all its figures.

Whole authentic profile photographs retain their source, creator, grant and existing download/delivery checksums. Photographs with contracted, partly buried, aquarium or preserved animals have those limits stated. Artist assets elsewhere in the archive are unchanged.

Checks and limitations

Full local regression passed 313 checks. Twenty affected checks passed after the final source-wording clarification. The first full local run passed 310 of 312, followed by specific anatomy-flag and revision-count repairs; a later full run passed 313 of 313 after the contact/tip corrections. Checks do not establish measured anatomy, full self-collision freedom, hydrodynamics or optical accuracy. Two initial new geometry checks found collapsed mound-cap triangles; the basal ring was repaired while preserving closed-edge and fixed-support requirements. Early drafts were rejected for straight needle-like tentacles, flat sparse plumose crowns and slow excessively dense preview geometry. Those drafts are excluded from accepted final counts.

Box-jellyfish comparison includes a privately inspected photograph credited Gavin Dally linked at the Australian Museum; its separate copyright is respected and the image is not redistributed. The retained whole profile reference depicts a preserved museum specimen. New Smithsonian comparative paper figure retrieval failed; only its accessible text is claimed. All final optical comparisons use software compatibility rendering.
