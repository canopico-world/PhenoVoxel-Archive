# Gray-whale correction v0.6.57

Model100A — Gray Whale, Digital Life / Johnson Martin, collaboration with Laurens Howle and Frank Fish, BOEM support. Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/model-100a-gray-whale-f59500b45e9143b399ed8f2c1357f66d . CC BY-NC4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/ . The supplied original20,494,184-byte GLB remains byte-exact, SHA256 `2ee3a1c8d10e28f8b3bfd9de012fc93311b5ec8812ef172903741e88952a10a0`. Creator describes a generic adult female reconstructed from images/video, not a living scan or accuracy certification. No online original-payload hash verification is claimed.

Active skin and baleen are separate derivatives of the artist atlas. Luminance-based neutralization removes conspicuous warm painted marks, with a selected lighter adult-gray transfer. New numerical grain, normal and roughness fields are unmeasured; the source has no normal map. Original eye atlas remains active. Original source images remain in the GLB. Exact active file hashes and algorithm are in `gray-whale-surface-v0657.json`. Derivative textures and source-based rendered/posed views retain CC BY-NC4.0. MIT software does not relicense them.

`gray-whale-skin.js` and193 low-profile, triangle-bound shell approximations are original PhenoVoxel Archive numerical geometry/software, MIT. They are qualitative shell exteriors with radial ridges and recessed centres, not a scanned parasite, census or complete Cryptolepas microanatomy. Selected shell scale, distribution,9.2-degree benthic aperture,27.5-degree oral-display aperture and16-second timings are unmeasured. Original source body/eye/baleen geometry, UVs and source topology remain intact.

Seventeen newly supplied comparison files remain private: repeated frames, one labeled illustration, Alamy/other watermarks and source captions are not redistribution grants. None are projected into textures or included in this public release. The retained whole Chris Foster reference photograph (`photo-eschrichtius-robustus.jpg`) remains separately CC BY4.0, https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/207073165 . No new original-resolution photograph retrieval is claimed. Comparison sheets keep its whole frame and separate attribution. Skin color, camera perspective and movement are not calibrated measurements.

Orange cyamids occur naturally. The selected no-red palette is not a claim that all real gray whales lack orange/yellow lice. Full swimming, suction, tongue/hyoid mechanics, contact, prey/sediment transport, filtration, swallowing and validated natural optics remain unfinished.
