# Beluga whale · v0.6.60

The active adult exterior, appendages, facial and oral surfaces, finite surface articulation, studio field and original generated/numerical skin maps are PhenoVoxel Archive contributions under the repository MIT license. This is an authored reconstruction, not a living scan or measured individual.

The separately retained starting reference is 3D Assets / beluga whale, CC0 1.0 Universal: https://3dassets.dev/assets/ocean-giants-kit-beluga-whale-5e58750f . Its contributor reports AI use. Original source SHA-256: 80a98d1efd0148bce949f0dedb233b122af104302fd1a8609da47489eca82dc9. Its bytes are unchanged. No original skin, skeleton or animation existed. The active continuous exterior is independently authored.

Original built-in ImageGen ivory pigment and two original model-guided material paintings were projected to the actual model. The saved maps include separate registered normals, roughness and local neutral occlusion calculated from actual triangle intersections. Exact original prompts and current image hashes are in beluga-surface-v0660.json. Generated material paintings are not photographs or 3D models. No supplied-photo pixels are included in the runtime or this package.

Free public reconstruction experiments produced no admitted replacement. The malformed InstantMesh result and restricted Tencent output are excluded. No paid 3D credits, purchase or subscription was used.

Neutral and oral GLBs preserve the final active mesh, normals, UVs, colors, materials and embedded maps. They are static selected poses and have no skeleton or animation clips. The interactive study supplies the seven bounded authored actions. Anatomy, material optics and timing remain unmeasured and unvalidated. Existing separately licensed whole reference photographs and primary biological figures retain their archive credits; none are included in the portable package.
