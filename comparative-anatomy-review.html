Inside a mouse and a human

Laboratory mouse

The body starts with a 28 g male nude mouse scanned by Dogdas, Stout, Chatziioannou and Leahy. MouseBio_3D repositioned the source specimen and supplied additional structures. The viewer retains all 434 component IDs. MouseBio_3D classifies 36 as source-labelled, 152 as traced and 246 as simulated. Read the description on an individual component to see how the authors divided an organ or estimated a boundary.

Choose a system, look up a structure and use its checkbox or the isolate control. Systems overlap. The connective-tissue filter includes the source-labelled names for cartilage, tendons and adipose/connective tissue. The six-second tail study applies the same positional deformation to tail surface, vessels and vertebral regions. This is an authored illustration of their spatial relationship. In-vivo timing, strain, blood flow and muscle activation await measurement.

The independent visual-cortex view contains 11,650 samples and 11,649 parent links from cell NMO_130632. The display rescales the tracing for inspection. Physical calibration and registration to this particular mouse are unresolved.

Human shoulder and senses

The right shoulder demonstration raises the arm through 24° of authored component rotations. The model combines a 2° clavicular elevation, a 4° scapular rotation and an 18° rotation of the humerus. The clavicle and scapula have separate pivots. Their contributions illustrate coupled movement documented in bone-fixed human measurements. The chosen angles and timing are teaching parameters. Bone shapes stay rigid and soft-tissue attachment is approximate.

The visual experiment reads the CIE standard photopic and scotopic luminous-efficiency tables at the selected wavelength. Each is normalized to its own maximum. The sound experiment computes the difference in arrival time at two points separated by 18 cm for a plane wave travelling at 343 m/s. Pathway cards cover touch and position, smell, taste, balance, cooling, itch, nociception and heartbeat awareness. Each links to evidence and a named gross anatomical structure. Phone RGB and microphone samples describe the device input.

Human mitochondrial record NC_012920.1 and mouse record NC_005089.1 have accession, taxonomy, source annotations and sequence checksums. Both are species references independent of the anatomical specimens.
