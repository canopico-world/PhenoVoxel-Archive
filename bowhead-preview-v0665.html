A mouth built for filtering

A large arched upper jaw supports two racks of narrow keratin plates. Fine fringes face the tongue. Bowheads feed with slow forward swimming, and research supports flow along as well as across the baleen. The skin’s irregular grey wear and white chin are a selected adult appearance; they vary among whales.

Life beside the ice

Elevated paired blowholes and thick insulating blubber suit Arctic life. The viewer shows external anatomy and selected postures. The linked report separates established anatomy from proposed mechanisms and the model’s remaining limits.
