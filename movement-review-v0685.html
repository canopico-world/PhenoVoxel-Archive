PHENOVOXEL · MOVEMENT STUDIES · v0.6.85

Inspect life in motion.

Twenty selectable studies across the twelve newest DigitalLife species and the flatback turtle. Each has finite playback, selectable phase times, automatic stopping, replay, and an exact return to its source pose.

13 species · 20 studies · 3 formerly static specimens given localized or articulated motion

Ten original creator sequences are retained. Gar and sturgeon turning and faster-swimming clips are now exposed. A great hammerhead can be examined during an authored roll into side swimming. The flatback uses its original skeleton for synchronous foreflipper strokes. The two resting frog scans have localized buccal-floor oscillations.

Scientific scope: these are external movement studies with illustrative timing. They do not establish measured gait, respiration, forces, energy use or complete physiology. Frog contact undersides were not photographed and remain reconstructed; their limbs are still in the source resting pose. Buccal movement is not a simulation of lung ventilation.

How to inspect: open a species, choose a study, and use its phase buttons under “Life, in motion.” Pause, select 0.25× playback, or enter a study time. The sequence stops at its end; Play replays it. Observe restores the source pose.
